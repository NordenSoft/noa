A question for the group, prompted by a mapping exercise we just completed with one consumer:

Consequential-action receipts from different producers carry parameter commitments that
cannot be compared, because each producer hashes its parameters under its own construction —
sometimes keyed. The result is that a cross-producer consumer can reach INDETERMINATE but
never EQUIVALENT, no matter how careful its harness is.

Is there interest from at least one additional independent ecosystem — beyond the parties
already in this exchange — in defining a versioned, cross-producer parameter-normalization
profile for consequential actions, with explicit construction identifiers carried on the wire
and conformance vectors as the acceptance test?

We are asking to gauge whether a second independent ecosystem wants this to exist. This is
not an announcement of an implementation commitment on our side.
